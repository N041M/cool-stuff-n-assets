Read me

Use it to show where you are, for example on a contact or about page. It sits in the page's flow as a figure and fills the width of its column. Visitors can turn it and zoom in as far as single countries.

Add it to your page

  1. Copy globe.js, globe.css and the data/ and fonts/ folders next to your page.

  2. Load them in the head.

    <link rel="stylesheet" href="globe.css">
<script defer src="globe.js"></script>

  3. Put the globe and its zoom buttons where it should appear.

    <figure class="fig globe-fig">
  <pre class="plot" data-figure="globe" data-world="data/world" aria-hidden="true"></pre>
  <div class="seg globe-zoom" role="group" aria-label="Globe">
    <button type="button" class="btn" data-globe="in" aria-label="Zoom in">+</button>
    <button type="button" class="btn" data-globe="out" aria-label="Zoom out">-</button>
    <button type="button" class="btn" data-globe="home" aria-label="Show Jablonec nad Nisou">JBC</button>
  </div>
</figure>

  4. To mark another place, change HOME near the top of globe.js and the label of the third button.

  5. Open the page from a web server, for example python3 -m http.server in its folder.

The full notes, options and API are in the README.
