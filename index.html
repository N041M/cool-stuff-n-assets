cool stuff n assets

This is code you can take and use in your own website. Open a demo to try it, then open its Read me to see which files to copy and the few lines that add it to your page. The code is under the MIT licence, so you can use it in any project.

  • 01Canvas 2DPixel driveAn endless generated drive in pixel art.
  • 02TextGlobeA text globe that can be dragged and zoomed.
  • 03TextText scrambleText that resolves out of random glyphs.
  • 04Canvas 2DPage backgroundThe glyph field behind the page, with a plot, a ruler and water.
  • 05Canvas 2DCursor trailThe glyphs the pointer leaves, and the ripple from a click.
  • 06WebGL2Gothic corridorA candlelit hall with a servitor and a censer, rendered live in WebGL2.
  • 07three.jsSTL modelsNine generated miniatures that can be turned, zoomed and downloaded.
  • 08WebGLSolar systemThe planets, their moons and the galaxy around them, drawn in text where they are right now.
  • 09CSS and SVGCRT bootA terminal screen that powers on, degausses and powers off like an old tube.
