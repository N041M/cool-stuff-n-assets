Use it as a page of its own where visitors explore space, from the planets where they are today out to the nearest stars and the whole galaxy, all drawn in text characters. It fills the window with two canvases and lays its controls over them.

Add it to your page

  1. The explorer combines the component folders beside it. To take one part on its own, start from that part's demo and its read-me.

    • Glyph renderer draws the character grid.
    • Planet positions works out where every body is.
    • Planet surfaces makes the maps of the bodies.
    • Night sky draws the background stars and galaxies.
    • Deep sky draws the galaxy, the nebulae and the black hole.
    • Comet draws Halley's coma and tails.
    • Spacecraft draws the spacecraft.

  2. For the whole explorer, copy the solar-system/ folder with its subfolders and keep their names. Serve it with a static web server and open demo.html.

    python3 -m http.server

  3. To build it into a page of your own instead, put the page in solar-system/ and load the components and the explorer at the end of the body.

    <script src="planet-positions/ephemeris.js"></script>
<script src="planet-surfaces/earth-map.js"></script>
<script src="planet-surfaces/surfaces.js"></script>
<script src="glyph-renderer/glyph-renderer.js"></script>
<script src="night-sky/night-sky.js"></script>
<script src="night-sky/night-sky-glyphs.js"></script>
<script src="deep-sky/deep-sky.js"></script>
<script src="deep-sky/deep-sky-glyphs.js"></script>
<script src="comet/comet.js"></script>
<script src="comet/comet-glyphs.js"></script>
<script src="spacecraft/spacecraft.js"></script>
<script src="spacecraft/spacecraft-glyphs.js"></script>
<script src="js/orrery.js"></script>

  4. Put two canvases before those scripts and fix them over the window. The second one holds the labels.

    <canvas id="sky"></canvas>
<canvas id="hud"></canvas>
<style>
  body { margin: 0; background: #050608; }
  #sky, #hud { position: fixed; top: 0; left: 0; display: block; }
</style>

  5. Start it after the scripts. Orrery.flyTo, Orrery.zoom and Orrery.rotate then move the camera from your own buttons, and Orrery.setRenderer("2d") draws the characters on a 2D canvas instead of with WebGL. Orrery.setGlyphs(false) draws the sky as a smooth picture without the characters.

    <script>
  Orrery.init(document.getElementById("sky"), document.getElementById("hud"), { focus: "earth" });
</script>

The explorer's own demo.js adds the body list, the info panel, the clock and the gestures. The full notes, options and API are in the README.
